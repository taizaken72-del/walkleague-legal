WalkLeague — Official Challenge Rules

Last updated: July 14, 2026

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A PURCHASE OR PAYMENT WILL NOT INCREASE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING.

1. Sponsor

These challenges are run by Tai Zaken, 4381 San Blas Ave, Los Angeles, CA 91364 (the “Sponsor”). Prizes may be provided by third-party brand sponsors, identified within the App for each challenge. Apple is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in, any challenge.

2. Eligibility

Open to legal residents of the United States and Canada who are 18 years or older at time of entry. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Employees of the Sponsor and their immediate families are not eligible.

3. Challenge period

Each challenge runs for the period stated in the App (e.g., a one-week cycle). Entry closes at the time shown in the App before each challenge begins. No entries are accepted after a challenge has started.

4. How to enter

  • Standard entry: Join an open challenge in the App during the entry window.
  • Free alternate method of entry (AMOE): No purchase or paid subscription is ever required to enter. Free-tier members may enter by inviting a friend who installs the App, as described in the App. This free method provides an entry equal to any other method.

Requiring a paid subscription is never the only way to enter a prize challenge — the free method above always remains available.

5. How winners are determined (skill-based)

Winning is based on skill and effort, not chance. The winner of each challenge group is the entrant with the highest WalkLeague score at the end of the challenge period, calculated from verified activity (steps, daily predictions, interval sessions, stairs, and streak bonuses) as described in the App. Ties are broken by the highest exact score, and if still tied, whichever entrant reached that score first. Activity is measured via Apple Health; Sponsor may disqualify entries it reasonably believes are fraudulent or manipulated.

6. Prizes

Prizes for each challenge are described in the App (for example, brand-sponsored cash or products), including their approximate retail value. Odds of winning depend on the number and performance of entrants. Prizes are non-transferable and may not be exchanged except at Sponsor’s discretion.

7. Winner notification & prize delivery

Winners will be notified in the App and/or by email. To claim a prize you may be required to verify eligibility and complete any required forms. For cash prizes, winners are responsible for all taxes, and U.S. winners receiving $600 or more in a year may be issued a Form 1099. Unclaimed prizes may be forfeited after 14 days.

8. Publicity

Except where prohibited, accepting a prize permits Sponsor to use the winner’s first name and city for promotional purposes without additional compensation.

9. General conditions

Sponsor may modify, suspend, or cancel a challenge if fraud, technical failures, or other factors compromise its integrity. Sponsor’s decisions are final on all matters relating to the challenges.
